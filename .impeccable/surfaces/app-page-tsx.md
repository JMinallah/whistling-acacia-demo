## Scope

Single-page marketing site for Whistling Acacia Guesthouse (fictional Kampala B&B, portfolio/client-acquisition demo). Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job, action, constraints

Prospective guests on a phone browser deciding whether to contact/book a small Kampala guesthouse. Job: judge location, rooms/price, trust, and reach a human fast. Primary action: WhatsApp or tap-to-call enquiry. Content is entirely fictional demo data (see PRODUCT.md / docs/blueprint.md) — no real photography exists, so the surface must carry emotional weight through authored pattern/illustration rather than photos.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page trades the generic "hotel template" (photo-hero, icon-grid amenities, card-grid rooms) for a guesthouse that presents itself the way its own hospitality does — through Buganda barkcloth (olubugo) craft: warm hand-worked terracotta material, stitched seams, torn organic edges — rather than a stock-photo cover image standing in for a place with no photos yet.

OWN-WORLD: Ground is warm terracotta/rust bark tone (not cream) carrying 40–50% of the surface in banded sections alternating with a warm bone-paper ground; ink is deep charcoal-brown, never pure black; one accent, a muted acacia-gold, reserved for primary CTAs and active states only. Structure reads as stitched barkcloth patchwork: panel seams rendered as fine dashed stitch-lines, section edges given a subtle torn/organic silhouette (irregular clip-path, not a geometric wave), cards as sewn patches with soft cloth-shadow rather than flat drop-shadow. Display type: Newsreader (warm literary serif with real italics) for headlines; body/UI in Plus Jakarta Sans. Corner language: soft but irregular, never a uniform 8px radius everywhere — patch corners feel hand-cut.

STORY: Visitor lands, immediately reads what the place is, where (Naguru hill, Kampala), and gets one unmistakable next step (WhatsApp). Scrolls through quick trust facts, rooms they can compare at a glance, amenities, an editorial "gallery" built from authored pattern plates (since no real photos exist — labelled honestly), location, sample guest impressions clearly marked as demo content, and a final conversion band repeating the WhatsApp/call CTA.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed terracotta hero band. Left-aligned (stacks centered on mobile): small wordmark top-left, one-line value proposition in Newsreader italic display, location line, primary WhatsApp CTA + secondary "Call" CTA side by side. Right side (desktop) or below (mobile): an authored barkcloth-patchwork illustration panel (stitched geometric patches, torn top edge bleeding into the bone-paper section below) standing in for a photograph — no stock photo, no gradient-blob filler. Nav sits above the band, transparent over terracotta, condensing on scroll.

FORM: Committed color strategy (terracotta ground carries the surface, not a neutral-plus-accent restraint) fused with an editorial hospitality-brochure structure (stitched dividers, torn section edges, authored plates in place of photography). Chosen directly against the blueprint's explicit brief ("warm, calm, premium, trustworthy, one restrained accent, avoid generic hotel-template") rather than through the tool's automated multi-candidate roll, since no image generation is available in this session to run its comp-led pipeline and the interactive decision-page round was judged disproportionate to a single small-business demo page — disclosed to the user rather than run silently.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
